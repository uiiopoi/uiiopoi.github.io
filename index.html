Research研究

Co-location as an Alternative to Waiting for Grid Interconnection以共址替代等待并网

Working paper · UC Berkeley SPUR, Energy Institute · 2026, in progress工作论文 · 伯克利 SPUR 本科研究，能源研究所 · 2026 年，进行中

Renewable projects in California's interconnection queue often wait years for a grid connection. The paper asks whether a developer should build anyway and sell power behind the meter to an adjacent AI data center in the meantime, and at what price both sides come out ahead. Four linked models cover wholesale prices, project cash flows with tax equity, the data center's delivered cost, and bilateral pricing.

加州并网队列里的可再生能源项目往往要等好几年才能接入电网。论文研究的是：开发商是否应该先建起来，在等待期间把电表后直供给相邻的 AI 数据中心？在什么价格下双方都能获益？研究由四个联动模型组成：批发电价、含税收股权的项目现金流、数据中心的到户成本，以及双边定价。
