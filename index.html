About关于我

I'm an undergraduate at UC Berkeley studying Environmental Economics & Policy, with a minor in Energy & Resources, after two years at Sciences Po Paris through the dual-degree program.

我在加州大学伯克利分校读环境经济与政策专业，辅修能源与资源。此前通过双学位项目在巴黎政治学院学习了两年。

My work sits where energy markets meet project finance. I want to know when renewable projects, storage and data centers make financial sense, and who captures the value. I've worked on these questions at a data center developer, a climate venture fund, and research institutes in Beijing.

我关注能源市场与项目融资的交汇处：可再生能源、储能和数据中心什么时候在财务上可行，价值又归谁所有。我在数据中心开发商、气候风投基金和北京的研究机构都研究过这些问题。

Research研究

UC Berkeley SPUR · Energy Institute · 2026伯克利 SPUR 本科研究 · 能源研究所 · 2026

Co-location as an Alternative to Waiting for Grid Interconnection 以共址替代等待并网：AI 数据中心与排队中可再生能源项目的经济性

Working paper, in progress · Mar 2026 – present工作论文（进行中）· 2026 年 3 月至今

Renewable projects in California's interconnection queue often wait years for a grid connection. I ask whether they should build early and sell power behind the meter to an adjacent AI data center in the meantime, and at what price both sides come out ahead. 加州并网队列里的可再生能源项目往往要等好几年才能接入电网。我研究的问题是：它们是否应该提前建设，在等待期间把电表后直供给相邻的 AI 数据中心？在什么价格下双方都能获益？
